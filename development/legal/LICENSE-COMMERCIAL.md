## 1. Principle (Default = Prohibited)

1. JiMesh is licensed under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0: use,
   modification, and redistribution are permitted **for noncommercial
   purposes only**.
2. **Any commercial use is prohibited without the rights holder's written
   permission.**
3. **No rule = no right:** if this file or any other written grant does not
   expressly permit a commercial use, or if no agreement with the
   operator/rights holder is reached, commercial use is **not allowed**.
   Silence, the absence of a rule, or non-response is **not** consent.

## 2. What counts as commercial use

The clauses "Noncommercial Purposes", "Personal Uses", and "Noncommercial
Organizations" of `LICENSE` are authoritative. Rule of thumb: commercial is
anything intended to make money or serving a revenue-generating context — for
example JiMesh as part of a product, an internal business tool, a hosted
service, or paid consulting/training built on it. When in doubt, §1.3 applies
(no written agreement = prohibited).

## 3. Ways to obtain commercial usage rights

Commercial use is permitted exclusively through one of these routes:

1. **Direct contract with the rights holder** — an individual written
   agreement (scope of use, fees, optionally equity/revenue share, resale
   and packaging rights). This applies expressly to contributors who want
   equity in the product or the right to resell it: **only** via a direct
   contract.
2. **Purchased commercial license (paid tiers)** — if and once offered.
   **No commercial licenses are currently offered**; purchasing one is not
   possible today (route 1 is the only option).
3. **Additional written grants** — exceptions documented by the rights
   holder, for example as a dedicated license or mandate file in the
   repository or as a clause in a contract. Until anything of the kind
   exists: prohibited.

The PolyForm license itself does **not** allow assignment or sublicensing
("No Other Rights") — commercial rights therefore arise exclusively from the
written agreements described in this section.

## 4. Contributors

1. Contributions are accepted only under the terms of
   [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md): contributors grant the rights holder
   comprehensive exploitation rights for both licensing sides — otherwise
   commercial licensing of the project as a whole would be legally
   impossible.
2. **Without a direct contract**, a contributor acquires **no** commercial
   rights through their contribution: no resale, no packaging, no equity
   in the product.
3. Equity, revenue shares, or resale rights for contributors exist
   exclusively through a **direct contract** with the rights holder (§3.1).
